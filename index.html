CyberGuard: AI-powered cyber threat detection and response

Phishing, impersonation, account takeover and unusual API activity can slip past rule-based security, and analysts get too many alerts with too little context. CyberGuard is a near-real-time system that tells security teams what is dangerous, why, and what to do next.

DetectAnalyseExplainRespond
  • Multi-source detection. Network and API activity, URLs, messages, identities and digital content.
  • AI threat classification. Flags phishing, impersonation, anomalies and other suspicious behaviour.
  • Risk scoring. Gives each threat a severity score so teams can prioritise.
  • Explainable AI. Shows why something was flagged and which indicators drove the decision.
  • Evidence-based analysis. Correlates behavioural, contextual and threat indicators.
  • Response recommendations. Suggests preventive and containment actions.
